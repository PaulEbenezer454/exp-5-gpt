SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between ABC Technologies ("Client") and XYZ Solutions ("Service Provider").

1. PURPOSE
The Service Provider agrees to provide software development and maintenance services to the Client.

2. OBLIGATIONS
The Service Provider shall develop the agreed software features and provide monthly progress updates. The Client shall provide project requirements and necessary feedback in a timely manner.

3. PAYMENT
The Client shall pay the Service Provider INR 50,000 per month. Payment is due within 15 days after receipt of the monthly invoice.

4. DURATION
This Agreement begins on 1 October 2026 and continues for 12 months unless terminated earlier according to this Agreement.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties shall keep confidential information private and shall not disclose it to third parties without written permission, except where disclosure is required by law.

6. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 days' written notice to the other party.

END
